Shipping Policy

AKANESHI ships across India using a secure fulfillment workflow. Orders are usually processed within 2–5 business days after payment verification and stock confirmation.

Standard shipping charges apply to orders below a defined threshold, while premium orders may qualify for free delivery. Exact shipping fees and delivery windows may vary depending on the product and destination.

Customers will receive tracking updates once the order is dispatched. AKANESHI is committed to secure packaging and reliable delivery updates.
